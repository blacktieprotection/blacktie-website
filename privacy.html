Privacy

Website Privacy Notice

Black Tie Protection treats privacy as part of professional conduct. This notice explains the limited information collected through this development-stage website.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Information submitted through the website

When you use the website inquiry form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, general location, inquiry type, and the message you choose to send.

The public inquiry form is intended only for initial contact. Please do not submit sensitive protective information, threat details, home addresses, itineraries, family information, government identification information, medical information, or other confidential operational details through the website.

How submitted information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to review and respond to inquiries, professional introductions, partnership communications, and other legitimate business contact.

Black Tie Protection does not sell website-submitted personal information.

Website form provider

The current website uses Formspree to process inquiry-form submissions. Information submitted through the form may therefore be transmitted through and processed by that provider subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

Development-stage website

Black Tie Protection is currently in development and is not accepting protective engagements through this website. The website and its information-handling practices may change as the company builds its launch-ready operating systems, contracts, privacy controls, and secure client workflows.

Security and confidentiality

No public web form should be treated as an appropriate channel for highly sensitive protective information. When a future matter requires confidential or operationally sensitive discussion, an appropriate private communication method should be established before those details are exchanged.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be submitted through the private inquiry form.

Professional review

This development-stage notice is an interim website disclosure and is not intended to replace the formal privacy, data-retention, contractual, and client-information policies that will be developed and professionally reviewed before operational launch.
