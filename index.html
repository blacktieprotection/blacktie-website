Why Black Tie Protection

Judgment matters more than visibility.

Effective protection should support the client’s life instead of taking it over. BTP’s operating model is being built around six principles.

Discretion

Low-profile service, professional boundaries, and respect for private information are foundational—not optional add-ons.

Continuity

Familiarity with the client, preferences, schedule, and environment supports smoother and more consistent protection.

Professional Judgment

Staffing and protective posture are driven by the engagement, risk, complexity, and jurisdiction—not by theatrics.

Controlled Capacity

Lawful Mobility

Client Fit

About BTP

A private-client protection firm being built deliberately.

Black Tie Protection, LLC is a Michigan-based company in development. Its founder brings a professional public-safety and law-enforcement background to a business being designed around preparation, discretion, calm decision-making, and disciplined client service.

BTP is independently owned and developed. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any government or law-enforcement agency.

Future Operating Footprint

Michigan first. Protective travel supported responsibly.

BTP’s planned primary market is Michigan. Future U.S. travel will be supported through BTP’s own authority where applicable and vetted, properly authorized local partners when required by jurisdiction.
