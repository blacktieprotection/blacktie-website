Services

Protective Services Designed Around Real Need.

BTP is being built around four focused service lines. The right structure for a future engagement will depend on the principal, environment, exposure, travel, timing, jurisdiction, and actual risk—not a one-size-fits-all package.

Private Inquiry Our Approach
01

Executive Protection

Discreet protection integrated into the client’s life.

Executive Protection is BTP’s core service. Future engagements may support private clients, families, founders, business owners, and executives whose profile, schedule, circumstances, or exposure justify dedicated protective support.

The protective posture is intended to remain low-profile whenever possible. Staffing, planning, movement, liaison, and protective presence will be based on the engagement rather than a predetermined image of what executive protection should look like.

  • Principal-focused protective coverage
  • Advance and movement planning
  • Venue and stakeholder coordination
  • Protective structure matched to risk and complexity
02

Protective Travel

Continuity at home and responsible support while traveling.

Protective Travel is being designed for clients whose protective needs move with them. BTP’s planned model prioritizes continuity while respecting state-specific licensing, credentialing, insurance, contracting, and firearms requirements.

For U.S. travel outside BTP’s direct authority, future assignments may incorporate vetted, properly authorized local partners while preserving the client relationship and continuity role where lawful and appropriate.

  • Travel-day and transition planning
  • Hotel, venue, airport, and transportation coordination
  • Jurisdiction-specific authorization review
  • Vetted local partner support when required
03

Private Event & Short-Term Protection

Focused coverage for defined periods of exposure.

Not every client needs an ongoing protection program. This service is intended for private events, public appearances, meetings, sensitive transitions, short trips, or other situations where a defined period calls for professional protective support.

Scope and staffing will be based on the environment, attendance, protectees, public exposure, movement, and other relevant factors. BTP will not under-staff an engagement solely to meet a budget target.

  • Private events and appearances
  • Short-duration protective assignments
  • Arrival, movement, and departure coordination
  • Scalable staffing based on actual requirements
04

Retained Protective Services

Reserved capacity and continuity for ongoing needs.

Retained Protective Services are being designed for clients who value priority access, familiarity, continuity, and ongoing planning rather than arranging each engagement from the beginning.

A retainer is intended to reserve protective capacity—not merely purchase a block of hours. Future retainer structures will be offered selectively based on client need, operational fit, capacity, and applicable legal requirements.

  • Priority access to reserved protective capacity
  • Greater continuity and familiarity
  • Ongoing planning for recurring needs
  • Selective acceptance based on fit and capacity

Armed & Unarmed Capability

Capability follows risk, law, and operational need.

BTP is being structured to support armed executive protection where legally authorized, properly licensed, operationally appropriate, and consistent with the client’s risk profile. An armed posture is not automatic, and personal carry authority is not treated as a substitute for the credentials required to perform paid protective work.

A Deliberate Standard

Protection is not sold by the hour alone.

Future staffing recommendations will consider threat, complexity, number of protectees, exposure, travel, duration, jurisdiction, and the environment around the client.

How Future Engagements Will Begin

Private consultation before protective commitment.

A future engagement will begin with a confidential conversation to understand scope, timing, location, client fit, and the general nature of the concern. Sensitive operational information will move to an appropriate private channel rather than being collected through a public web form.
